I'm a Mobile Application Developer with **5+ years** of professional experience shipping cross-platform apps for fintech, government, and enterprise clients. I specialize in **Flutter & Dart**, applying Clean Architecture, MVVM, and BLoC to deliver maintainable, high-performance products from concept to release.

- 🔭 Building production-grade mobile apps for fintech, government, and enterprise
- 🧩 Develop custom **Flutter plugins** with native **iOS (Swift)** and **Android (Kotlin/Java)** integrations
- 🧱 I care deeply about **architecture, testability, and developer experience**
- 📦 Open-source Flutter package author
- 🎓 BSc in Information Technology – Mobile Computing, University of Tripoli

### What I Bring

- **Cross-platform development** — single codebase for Android & iOS with native-quality UX
- **Flutter plugin development** — bridging Dart to native iOS (Swift) and Android (Kotlin/Java) via platform channels
- **Clean Architecture & MVVM** — clear separation of concerns and long-term maintainability
- **State management** — BLoC / Cubit patterns for predictable, scalable app state
- **Dependency Injection** — modular, testable, loosely-coupled code
- **API integration** — RESTful services, real-time data, and secure communication
- **Collaboration** — working with designers, backend teams, and stakeholders to ship on time
